Fizzy and My CCC privacy policy

Fizzy is the app Cooked Coke Co. staff use to run the counter: tabs, points and the stall's money. My CCC is the app students use to see their own points, tab and badges. Last updated 2 November 2026.

What Fizzy keeps

  • Customers: name, school email and student number.
  • What they buy from us, from our Square account, and the points, tab and repayments that go with it.
  • Staff names, and a log of the changes staff make.
  • Restocks, with any receipt photos staff add.
  • Questions staff ask on Fizzy's Ask page.

The My CCC app

My CCC shows a student their own points, tab, badges and recent activity, and the stall's leaderboard (first name and last initial only, and only for students who have a passcode). It doesn't collect anything new: it shows what Fizzy already keeps about that student. To sign in, the student types their name or student number and the passcode we gave them, or scans a one-time code at the counter. With "Stay signed in" on, the passcode is kept in the phone's Keychain so the app can sign in again by itself; signing out removes it. The app keeps a copy of the student's page on their phone so it opens with no signal.

Where it's kept

On Cooked Coke Co.'s own server, with a daily backup. The app keeps a copy of recently opened pages on the staff member's phone so they still open with no signal.

Who sees it

Only signed-in Cooked Coke Co. staff. Students can see their own points and tab in My CCC or on their own page on our website, and never anyone else's, apart from the leaderboard. We don't sell anything we keep, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else except as below. Neither app has ads, tracking or third-party analytics.

Other services

  • Square takes payments at the counter. Fizzy reads sales from our Square account.
  • Google Gemini answers questions on the Ask page. When staff ask one, the question and the stall data needed to answer it are sent to Google.

Removing your data

Ask anyone at the counter, or email contact@cookedcoke.co, and we'll remove your record. Sales stay in Square's records and our accounts, without your name on them.
