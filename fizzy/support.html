Fizzy and My CCC

Support

My CCC is the app students use to check their Cooked Coke Co. points, tab and badges. Fizzy is the app our staff use to run the counter.

My CCC

  • How do I sign in?Type your name or student number and your passcode. No passcode yet? Ask at the stall and we'll set one up. You can also tap Scan a sign-in code and scan the code staff show you at the counter.
  • I forgot my passcodeAsk at the stall. Staff can look it up or give you a new one.
  • My points or tab look wrongAsk us at the stall and we'll check what happened.

Fizzy, for staff

  • I can't sign inCheck you've picked your own name under "Who's on the counter", and ask the owner for the current staff password.
  • A restock says it's waitingThere was no signal when you saved it. It stays on your phone and is sent by itself once you're back online. You can see it at the top of Money.
  • A sale has no customer on itOpen the customer in Fizzy, tap Claim sale, then pick the sale.

Still stuck?

Ask us at the stall on Level 7, or send us an email. Our privacy policy covers what both apps keep.
