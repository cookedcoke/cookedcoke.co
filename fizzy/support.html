Fizzy and My CCC support

My CCC is the app students use to check their Cooked Coke Co. points, tab and badges. Fizzy is the app our staff use to run the counter.

Get help

Email contact@cookedcoke.co.

My CCC

How do I sign in?

Type your name or student number and your passcode. No passcode yet? Ask at the stall and we'll set one up. You can also tap Scan a sign-in code and scan the code staff show you at the counter.

I forgot my passcode

Ask at the stall. Staff can look it up or give you a new one.

My points or tab look wrong

Ask us at the stall and we'll check what happened.

Fizzy (staff)

I can't sign in

Check you've picked your own name under "Who's on the counter", and ask the owner for the current staff password.

A restock says it's waiting

There was no signal when you saved it. It stays on your phone and is sent by itself once you're back online. You can see it at the top of Money.

A sale has no customer on it

Open the customer in Fizzy and tap Claim sale, then pick the sale.
