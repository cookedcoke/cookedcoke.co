    Points and tabs

    Every time you buy something you earn points, and once you're a regular you can put things on a tab.

    Points1 point per cent

    1. Say your name when you pay. We add the points straight away. Spend $3, get 300.
    2. Swap them for rewards. Free drinks and money off. Ask at the stall when you've got enough.
    3. Look out for double points days. They show up at the top of this page and on our Discord.
    4. Keep a streak going. Buy something on days we're open to build a streak and earn badges.

    TabsPay within 7 days

    1. Buy now, pay later. Put a drink on your tab and pay it off at the stall within a week.
    2. Unlock it. Spend $30 with us and get your tab score to 60, then ask at the stall to switch it on.
    3. Your limit grows. It starts small and goes up as your tab score does.
    4. Pay on time. If a payment's late, your tab pauses until it's paid and your score drops.

    Your tab score is out of 100. It goes up when you spend with us, come back on different days and pay on time, and down when you pay late.
